How the implied value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity.

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap-per-TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category, read out of this snapshot (at least 3 peers, otherwise the snapshot-wide median). A DEX token trades at a different multiple from a lending token, and the snapshot itself says so. No protocol name is ever special-cased.
  • Growth — the protocol's 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%, at half weight: 1 + growth ÷ 100 × 0.5.
  • Maturity — 0.6 + age ÷ 1095 days × 0.4, so a protocol that has run three years with no token counts more than a new one. Unknown age counts as 365 days.

The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: every protocol on it is ranked, and the board adds the 160 pre-token protocols below the $50M floor with the most TVL — the early rows a radar exists to find. A protocol "records no token" in DefiLlama's record; that is not proof it has none. Live-token protocols are kept apart in their own view at their market caps. The implied value is this entry's own claim, never make.fun's.
